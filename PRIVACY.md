# Privacy Policy

**Tabletop Music Director** · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Tabletop Music Director does not collect, send or share any personal
information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking,
and it never connects to the internet.

## What stays on your device

Everything the app keeps is stored on your own computer or device.

### Windows and Linux

Everything is stored in the app's data folder:

| Platform | Folder |
|----------|--------|
| Windows | `%APPDATA%\TabletopMusicDirector\` |
| Linux | `~/.local/share/TabletopMusicDirector/` |

- **Your encounter library and settings** (`library.json`): encounter names,
  the locations of the music files you added, and your settings.
- **Log files** (the `logs` folder): technical messages from the app, which may
  include the locations of your music files. They are never sent anywhere.

The app plays your music files from where they are and never changes them.
It copies a file into your music folder only when you agree to it.

You can delete any of this at any time by deleting the data folder. The
installer asks whether to delete it when you uninstall. The Microsoft Store
version leaves it in place, so your library is still there if you reinstall;
delete the folder yourself if you no longer want it.

### Android

- **Your encounter library and settings** are stored in the app's private
  storage on the device, which no other app can read. Uninstalling the app
  deletes them.
- **Music files** are chosen with Android's own file picker, either as single
  files or a whole folder. The app keeps permission to read those files so they
  can play again after it restarts. It reads them only to play them. It does not
  copy, move, change or upload them, and it has no music folder.
- **Permissions.** The app asks for permission to read audio files on the
  device when it starts, and uses it only to find and play the music you choose.
  It also runs a media playback service, so the music keeps playing while the
  screen is off or the app is in the background, and it keeps the device awake
  while music plays. None of these sends anything off the device.

## Downloads and the app stores

GitHub, Google Play and the Microsoft Store handle downloads and installs under
their own privacy policies. Anything they collect, such as download counts or
crash reports the platform sends to them, goes to them, not to the app's
developer.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
