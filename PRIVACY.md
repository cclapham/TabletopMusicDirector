# Privacy Policy

**Tabletop Music Director** · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Tabletop Music Director does not collect, send or share any personal
information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking,
and it never connects to the internet.

## What stays on your computer

Everything the app keeps is stored on your own computer, in its data folder:

| Platform | Folder |
|----------|--------|
| Windows | `%APPDATA%\TabletopMusicDirector\` |
| Linux | `~/.local/share/TabletopMusicDirector/` |

- **Your encounter library and settings** (`library.json`): encounter names,
  the locations of the music files you added, and your settings.
- **Log files** (the `logs` folder): technical messages from the app, which may
  include the locations of your music files. They are never sent anywhere.

The app plays your music files from where they are and never changes them.
It copies a file into your music folder only when you agree to it.

You can delete any of this at any time by deleting the data folder. The
installer asks whether to delete it when you uninstall. The Microsoft Store
version leaves it in place, so your library is still there if you reinstall;
delete the folder yourself if you no longer want it.

## Downloads and the Microsoft Store

GitHub and the Microsoft Store handle downloads and installs under their own
privacy policies. Anything they collect, such as download counts or crash
reports Windows sends to Microsoft, goes to them, not to the app's developer.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an
